# Agent Templates

[Knowledge-Hub](../../README.md) / [.ai](../AI.md) / templates

Every new note is created by copying its template. Each template starts with the full front-matter metadata for its type (identity, facts, references to related notes, dates), as defined in section 2 of the [vault conventions](../skills/vault-conventions/SKILL.md) for business notes and rule 1 of [md-note-structure](../skills/md-note-structure/SKILL.md) for study notes.
