# Dashboards

[Knowledge-Hub](../README.md) / Dashboards

Dataview pages that query tasks, meetings, projects, and decisions. They never hold data of their own. Requires the Dataview plugin.

## Notes

- [Active Projects](Active_Projects.md) — projects by status, with their latest activity
- [Follow-Ups](Follow_Ups.md) — what you are waiting on, and what still needs a decision or a review
- [Open Tasks](Open_Tasks.md) — every open task in the vault
